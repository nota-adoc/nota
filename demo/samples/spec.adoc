= Technical specification: customer portal

== Goal

Build a *customer portal* where a client sees their _orders_, their statuses and the `waybill numbers`.
The deadline is **the end of the quarter**, the priority is high: without the portal, clients write to support.

== Requirements

The portal opens at https://portal.example.com and works in two modes: [.line-through]#sign-in by password# and #sign-in by code#.

Details are in the section <<acceptance,Acceptance>>, in the document xref:glossary.adoc#terms[glossary] and on the page link:legal/privacy.html[data policy].
Questions go to mailto:care@example.com[the support mailbox] or to <https://example.com/help>.

=== Example request

The request goes to the address *POST* `/api/orders`, and the answer carries the status of the order:

[source,json]
----
{"order": 42, "status": "shipped"}
----

==== Fields of the answer

The field `status` is one of the values _new_, _paid_, _shipped_ or __done__.

[[acceptance]]
== Acceptance

The portal is accepted when a client from the test group sees the orders **and** can open the card of any of them.
