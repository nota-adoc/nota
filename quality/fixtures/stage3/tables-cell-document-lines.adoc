= Lines in the document of a cell

The document in a cell of the `a` style is numbered from the line Ruby gives the cell, whatever line the text of it starts at.

A quoted CSV cell, a line break after the quote.

[cols="1a"]
,===
"
:===
x:y
:===
"
,===

A PSV cell, two blank lines, then the nested table.

[cols="1a"]
|===
a|


!===
!x !y
!===
|===

A PSV cell, blanks after the separator up to the end of the line.

[cols="1a"]
|===
a|
!===
!x !y
!===
|===

A quoted CSV cell, the nested table starts on the line of the quote.

[cols="1a"]
,===
"!===
!x !y
!==="
,===

A quoted CSV cell in the second column, a line break after the quote.

[cols="1,1a"]
,===
x,"
:===
x:y
:===
"
,===
